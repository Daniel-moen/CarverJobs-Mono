# CARVER — Superyacht crew, automated

> Text "match" — and get the yacht.

Carver runs the entire superyacht crew job hunt over WhatsApp. Build your profile, match against every live role, and apply — no app to install, no website to log into, no inbox to sort.

## What Carver does

- **Match:** Text `match` on WhatsApp and Carver scans every live role and returns the best fits for your profile.
- **Apply:** Reply with `apply <n>` and Carver submits the application on your behalf.
- **Jobs:** `jobs <role>` lists currently open berths by department.
- **Status:** `status` shows the state of your open applications.
- **CV:** `cv` returns your current CV; send a new file to replace it.
- **Pause / resume:** `pause` and `resume` stop and restart outreach.

## How it works

1. Open WhatsApp and send a message — no signup form, no app install.
2. Carver builds your crew profile from a short conversation.
3. Live listings from captains and verified agencies are matched against your profile.
4. You approve each application before it goes out.

## Trust & security

- End-to-end encrypted transport (TLS) and at-rest encryption for stored data.
- EU-hosted, GDPR-by-design.
- Real listings from captains and verified agencies only.
