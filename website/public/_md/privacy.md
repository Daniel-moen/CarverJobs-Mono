# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 May 2026. Privacy and data-rights requests: support@jobcarver.co_

## 1. Who we are

CARVER ("we", "us") operates this website and related services for superyacht crew job discovery and applications. Contact: support@jobcarver.co.

## 2. Information we collect

We may collect:

- Account details (e.g. email, username, authentication identifiers).
- Profile and career information you provide.
- Documents you upload where the product allows it.
- Usage data, device/browser type, and approximate logs (e.g. IP, timestamps) for security and operations.
- Communications you send us (e.g. support requests).

## 3. How we use information

We use data to provide and improve the service, authenticate users, process applications, communicate with you, comply with law, and protect against abuse.

## 4. Sharing

We may share data with service providers who assist us (e.g. hosting, email, analytics), when required by law, or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell your personal information as a commodity; if we use ad or analytics partners, their processing is governed by their policies and your settings where applicable.

## 5. Retention

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by law. You may request deletion as described on our data deletion page.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object or port data. Contact us at the email above. You may also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

## 8. International transfers

If we process data across borders, we use appropriate safeguards where required by law.

## 9. Children

The service is not directed at children under the age required in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the revised version here and adjust the "Last updated" date.
